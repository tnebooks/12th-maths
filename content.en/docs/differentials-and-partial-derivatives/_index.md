It covers the computation of first and higher-order partial derivatives, the chain rule for multivariable functions, and the concept of differentials for approximating changes in functions. The chapter also discusses Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions, the total differential, and applications such as finding tangent planes, linear approximations, and solving problems in thermodynamics and economics."
