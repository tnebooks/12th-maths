**If $|\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj}A)|=|A|^9$, then the order of the square matrix $A$ is**

```
Let the order of the square matrix be $n$.

For an $n\times n$ matrix,

$$
|\operatorname{adj}(A)|=|A|^{n-1}
$$

Applying the same property to $\operatorname{adj}(A)$,

$$
|\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj}A)|
=|\operatorname{adj}(A)|^{n-1}
=\left(|A|^{n-1}\right)^{n-1}
=|A|^{(n-1)^2}
$$

Given,

$$
|A|^{(n-1)^2}=|A|^9
$$

Hence,

$$
(n-1)^2=9
$$

$$
n-1=3
$$

$$
n=4
$$

Therefore, the order of the matrix is **4**.
```
