**If $P=\begin{bmatrix}1&x&0\\1&3&0\\2&4&-2\end{bmatrix}$ is the adjoint of a $3\times3$ matrix $A$ and $|A|=4$, then $x$ is**

```
Since $P=\operatorname{adj}A$ and $A$ is $3\times3$,

$$
|P|=|A|^{3-1}=4^2=16.
$$

Expanding along the third column,

$$
|P|=-2\begin{vmatrix}1&x\\1&3\end{vmatrix}
=-2(3-x)=2x-6.
$$

So,

$$
2x-6=16 \Rightarrow x=11.
$$
```
