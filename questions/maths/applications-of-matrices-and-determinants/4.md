**If $A\begin{bmatrix}1&-2\\1&4\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}6&0\\0&6\end{bmatrix}$, then $A=$**

```

$$
A\begin{bmatrix}1&-2\\1&4\end{bmatrix}=6I_2
$$


we get


$$
A=6\left(\begin{bmatrix}1&-2\\1&4\end{bmatrix}\right)^{-1}.
$$


Since


$$
\det\begin{bmatrix}1&-2\\1&4\end{bmatrix}=6,
$$


its inverse is


$$
\frac{1}{6}\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\-1&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$


Therefore,


$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\-1&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$


Hence, the correct answer is **(3)**.
```
