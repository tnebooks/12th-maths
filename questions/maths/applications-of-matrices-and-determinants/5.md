**If $A=\begin{bmatrix}7&3\\4&2\end{bmatrix}$, then $9I_2-A=$**

```
For the given matrix,


$$
9I_2-A=\begin{bmatrix}2&-3\\-4&7\end{bmatrix}.
$$


Also,


$$
A^{-1}=\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix}2&-3\\-4&7\end{bmatrix}.
$$


Hence,


$$
9I_2-A=2A^{-1}.
$$

```
