**If $A$, $B$ and $C$ are invertible matrices of some order, then which one of the following is not true?**

```
The adjugate of a product reverses the order:

$$
\operatorname{adj}(AB)=\operatorname{adj}(B)\operatorname{adj}(A).
$$

So the statement

$$
\operatorname{adj}(AB)=(\operatorname{adj}A)(\operatorname{adj}B)
$$

is not true in general.
```
