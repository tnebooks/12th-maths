**If $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\1&5\end{bmatrix}$ and $B=\begin{bmatrix}1&4\\2&0\end{bmatrix}$, then $|\operatorname{adj}(AB)|=$**

```
For 2\times2 matrices,

$$
|\operatorname{adj}(AB)|=|AB|=|A||B|.
$$

Now,

$$
|A|=10,\qquad |B|=-8.
$$

Therefore,

$$
|\operatorname{adj}(AB)|=10\cdot(-8)=-80.
$$
```
